Round one drew a landscape app with a title bar and a side rail. That isn't how it will live on your desktop. Your Hyprland config tiles windows into tall columns, so this round designs HyprKvm at your two most likely column sizes, 279 and 874 pixels wide and 1428 tall. It uses the phone's model: one scrolling column of cards, tiles that open screens, and Back.

01Built for your columns.

Every mock below is drawn at real pixels inside your 2 px border. Super+W cycles between the sizes, so both have to work.

02Nothing the phone doesn't have.

Hyprland draws no title bar and HyprKvm adds none. There's no rail. The brand card is the top of the window.

03Tiles go forward, Back comes home.

Home is a scroll of cards. A tile opens a screen, and that screen's header has Back. Esc and the mouse's back button do the same.

04Red means live.

Red marks a stream, captured keys or a destructive action, and nothing else.

00

Your columns

DP-1 is 3440 × 1440 and uses the scrolling layout. Your Ember bar takes 40 px on the left, gaps are 4 px, and borders are 2 px. column_widths.lua sets three Super+W presets as fractions of the usable width.

Narrow
0.0858 × 3392 ≈ 279 × 1428 inside the border
Default
0.2616 × 3392 ≈ 874 × 1428. New windows open at this size, and it matches the 874 × 1428 kitty window measured in the receiver docs
Wide
0.3721 × 3392 ≈ 1250 × 1428. The default layout stretches to fit it
  • Every size is portrait. The phone's single column is the right starting point, not a landscape app
  • 279 px is narrower than the phone's 412 dp, so the narrow layout is the phone layout made more compact
  • 874 px fits two phone-width columns side by side
  • On DP-2 (portrait, dwindle) a half-split window is about 1432 × 1276, so it gets the two-column layout too

Use Mock size in the bar above to see every window whole. Actual shows them at real pixels.

01

Home is the whole app

The phone app has no settings screen. Its settings are cards further down the home scroll. HyprKvm works the same way: status first, then every setting from today's Settings window, as cards.

Top
The brand card, then the hero: Tera, LINKED, address, round trip and uptime
Tiles
View opens its screen. Control and Clipboard flip in place. Notifications scrolls to its card
Cards
Arrangement, Sharing, Devices, View, Notifications, This computer and Advanced. Each card's label is its heading
Save
A bar floats in only when something needs a restart, with Discard and Save
279
One column. Rows stack, with the label above the control. Tiles are 2 × 2 at about 124 px
874
Two columns: status on the left and settings on the right, each about the phone's width
Getting around
Tiles and buttons go forward, and Back in a header goes home. Esc and mouse back also go home. Nothing needs a side panel
  • One screen to learn, the same as on the phone
  • At 874 almost everything is visible without scrolling
  • At 279 the settings are a long scroll below the tiles

Both windows share one state. Change Audio in either and the tile follows. Open View from one and both go to it, then press Back.

02

View: a column watching a column

Tera's windows are the same tall columns as yours, so a remote kitty column fits a default-width View almost 1:1. The screen is the phone's: a header, then a full-bleed frame. At 279 it keeps the phone's dock, and at 874 the controls move into the header.

Header
Back, then the live dot and window title (opens the window picker), then the Adjust/Interact switch. At 874 it also holds Text/Media, Fit/Fill/1:1, sound and keys
Dock (279)
Text/Media and Fit, then a Super group of four arrows and the keys button, then stream stats
Adjust
Drag pans, the wheel scrolls the picture, and double-click swaps Fill and Fit. Nothing reaches Tera
Interact
A red outline. Clicks, right-clicks and the wheel go to Tera. Clicking the frame captures the keyboard, and Ctrl + Alt releases it
  • The same controls in the same order as the phone, so habits carry over
  • Super+W on the View window makes the picture bigger with no other change
  • At 279 a remote column shows at about 0.3× in Fit. Fill and panning are the useful mode there
  • Should crossing into Tera by the screen edge drop an open View to Adjust?

Flip Interact on, click the frame and type, then press Ctrl + Alt. Use the Super arrows to follow Firefox and Files. Esc goes back when the keys aren't captured.

03

Pairing popups

Pairing happens in small popups, not inside the app column. They're separate floating windows, as today, so they appear over whatever you're doing. That includes when the tray's "Add phone…" opens one or another computer asks to pair. There's no title bar: Hyprland's border frames them, and ✕ or Esc closes them. The thing you check is the biggest thing on screen: the QR code, or the six digits in dot-matrix type.

Add a phone
380 × 520. A live countdown, the QR code on a white card, and a blinking dot while waiting
Confirm
520 × 520. "Pair with Tera?", the code, then the fingerprints on request. A changed key adds a red-outlined warning and "Pair anyway"
Opened from
The Devices card on Home, the tray, or an incoming pairing request
  • They open centred over your current column, whatever width it is
  • They work even when the HyprKvm window isn't open
  • They need a Hyprland rule: float and centre for the hyprkvm-pair class

On Home, click "+ Add phone" or "Pair a computer" in Devices to see the popup float over the column.

To decide

Open questions

egui or Tauri + Svelte

One scrolling column of cards is exactly HyprMobile's page. With Tauri, the cards, tiles and theme could be shared rather than redrawn.

Lean: Tauri on a migration branch.

Save, or apply as you go

The phone applies changes immediately. Connection settings here restart HyprKvm, so they wait for Save. Sharing could apply live.

Lean: live for Sharing and View, Save only for This computer.

Open View in its own window

A second HyprKvm window just for View would let you keep Home in one column and the picture in a wider one beside it.

Lean: a "View in new column" action, later.

The release chord

Ctrl + Alt is familiar from VMs but collides with AltGr on your GB layout.

Lean: Right Ctrl, changeable in the View card.
